# 🎉 Milestone Celebration AR - Quick Start (2 Minutes)

**Get celebrating in under 2 minutes!**

---

## ⚡ Instant Demo

### Option 1: Direct Access (Fastest)
1. **Open browser**: `http://localhost:3000/milestone-celebration`
2. **Allow camera** when prompted
3. **You're celebrating!** 🎊

### Option 2: From Milestone Tracker
1. **Navigate to**: `http://localhost:3000/milestones`
2. **Check any milestone checkbox**
3. **Automatic celebration launches!** 🎉

---

## 🎨 Try All 5 Themes

Click each celebration style at the bottom:
- 🎊 **Confetti** - Colorful party pieces
- 🎆 **Fireworks** - Explosive effects
- ⭐ **Stars** - Twinkling sparkles
- 💖 **Hearts** - Floating love
- 🎈 **Balloons** - Rising bubbles

---

## 📸 Capture a Photo

1. **Position your camera** (point at child or yourself)
2. **Choose your favorite theme**
3. **Click big green "Capture Celebration" button**
4. **Download or save to album**

---

## 🔄 Controls

- **🔄 Flip Camera**: Switch front/back camera
- **↻ Refresh**: Clear particles and restart
- **✕ Close**: Exit celebration

---

## ✅ What You'll See

✨ **Full-screen camera feed**  
🎊 **Animated particles falling/rising**  
📝 **Milestone info at top**  
🎯 **Milestone details card (bottom-left)**  
🎨 **Theme selector (bottom)**  
📸 **Capture button (center)**

## 📱 Mobile Testing

### Android Chrome:
1. Same as above
2. May need to enable camera in settings first

---

## 🐛 Issues?

### Camera not working?
- ✅ Check browser permissions
- ✅ Use HTTPS (or localhost)
- ✅ Try different browser

### Slow performance?
- ✅ Choose simpler theme (hearts/stars)
- ✅ Click refresh button
- ✅ Close other apps

### Can't capture photo?
- ✅ Wait for camera to fully load
- ✅ Ensure green button is visible
- ✅ Try again after refresh
