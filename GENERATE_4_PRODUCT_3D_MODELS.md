### Step 2: Generate 3D Models Using Meshy.ai

**For EACH of the 4 products, do this:**

#### A. Go to Meshy.ai
1. Visit: **https://www.meshy.ai**
2. Click **"Sign Up"** (it's FREE)
3. Sign up with Google or Email
4. You get **10-20 FREE credits** to start

#### B. Upload Product Image
1. Click **"Create New"**
2. Select **"Image to 3D"**
3. Click **"Upload Image"**
4. Select your product image
5. **IMPORTANT**: Select **"Product/Object"** mode (not character)
6. Click **"Generate"**

#### C. Wait for Generation (2-5 minutes)
- You'll see a progress bar
- Don't close the tab
- Usually takes 2-5 minutes per model

#### D. Download the 3D Model
1. Once done, click **"Download"**
2. Select **"GLB"** format (NOT GLTF, NOT FBX)
3. Save with a descriptive name like:
   - `feeding-set-blue-dishes.glb`
   - `baby-bottles-colorful-cups.glb`
   - `baby-shoes-pink-bow.glb`
   - `baby-shoes-pink-plate.glb`
